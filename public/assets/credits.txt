RÉMANENCE — CRÉDITS VISUELS

Ce site est une création de démonstration. Rémanence est une identité
provisoire. Le portfolio ne présente ni commandes ni références clients réelles.

IMAGES GÉNÉRÉES

Mode : outil intégré de génération d’images (ImageGen).
Les images ont été générées pour cette création, puis compressées en WebP.

1. Lumière intérieure — assets/hero.webp

Prompt :
Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: full-bleed hero photograph for a
luxury French photography studio website, landscape 3:2 composition. Create a
stunning cinematic editorial 35mm photograph, beautifully human and natural.
A young adult Black woman with short natural hair and luminous dark brown skin
in an ivory silk sleeveless top, photographed in profile and three-quarter view,
eyes gazing softly to the left. She stands in a shadowy artist's studio near a
tall window, warm late afternoon light grazing her face and shoulder. Subject in
the RIGHT THIRD of the image, medium close portrait from chest up, head fully
inside frame, large dark near-black negative space on the LEFT HALF for a large
white headline. The atmosphere is quiet, intimate, confident; warm film
highlights, subtle grain, rich deep shadows, muted neutral tones, extraordinary
skin texture and sophisticated composition. A soft gauzy window curtain catches
a sliver of light at the far right. This is a photographic agency's own artistic
portfolio, not corporate stock. No text, no logos, no graphic overlay, no borders,
no watermark, no 3D, no illustration. High resolution.

2. La beauté du geste — assets/gestes.webp

Prompt :
Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: portfolio photograph for a refined
cinematic photography studio website, landscape 3:2. An intimate documentary
photograph of a ceramic artisan's hands shaping a large wet clay bowl on a
potter's wheel inside a real working studio. Close medium framing at wheel
height, focus on the expressive hands covered in clay and the beautifully
simple vessel, the artisan's plain oatmeal linen apron in the background, face
out of frame. Tall window light from the left draws warm natural highlights on
clay, soft deep shadows and bokeh shelves of earthenware in the background.
Sophisticated warm-neutral 35mm color film aesthetic, rich near-black browns,
taupe and muted cream, subtle grain, soft highlights, authentic tactile
materials. Editorial, observational, beautifully composed, entirely realistic.
No bright colors, no commercial stock-photo posing, no logos, no text,
no watermarks, no illustrations, no 3D. High resolution.
